## Project Theme
Our project is a car rental website that acts as a service for a fictional business called 'DriveAway'. The website provides a simple, accessible and professional platform for browsing and renting vehicles for various purposes such as events, trips or temporary replacements.

Implemented features include; login and sign-up page, main home page, about us page, a contact form, membership portal, car selection page and booking form. 

The website allows users to create an account or sign into an existing account to use the site. An account is used for storing past and present car rentals that are booked.

From there, the users can view a range of cars available for rental on the rental page and select a suitable vehicle for their needs, before filling out a booking form. The booking form collects necessary user information and validates it to ensure correctness. Users can subsequently view their prior and current bookings through the membership portal.

The contact form is used to serve and help users who are having issues and wish to contact the business for support with queries.

The membership portal gives users control over their account information including changing their email and password, and also for adjusting settings like theme (light or dark) for accessibility and personalisation.

There is also an 'About' page that displays information about the business including what services are provided, company values and reasons to choose DriveAway.
